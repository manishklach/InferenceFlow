# Internal pricing experiment
This is not a public benchmarking product. Test matched pinned model/revision/template/decoding and quality across classification, extraction and summarization, with input/output length and burst distributions recorded without content. Measure 48 hours at 10/30/60/80% occupancy, including cold starts, idle GPU, retries, CPU/storage/egress and payment fees. Compare like-for-like quality and completion targets. Revenue derives from actual successful token captures; costs include unsuccessful attempts and idle time. Reconcile paid GPU seconds and invoices. Publish no cost advantage or token rate until positive contribution margin and deadline reliability are demonstrated.
